Common questions

How long does a water heater installation take?

A standard tank replacement takes about 2-4 hours. Tankless installations may take longer depending on gas line and venting requirements.

What size water heater do I need?

We'll calculate based on your household size and usage patterns. Most homes need a 40-50 gallon tank or a tankless unit with adequate flow rate.
