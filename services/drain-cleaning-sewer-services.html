Drain Cleaning & Sewer Services

Professional drain cleaning and sewer line repair in Dallas.

Schedule this service

Overview

Slow drains and sewer backups are more than a nuisance – they can damage your home. Our advanced drain cleaning equipment clears blockages quickly, and we offer trenchless sewer repair to minimize disruption to your yard.

What's included

  • Hydro-jetting for stubborn clogs
  • Camera inspection to find the exact problem
  • Trenchless sewer repair (no digging)
  • Preventative maintenance plans available

How it works

1

Camera Inspection

We run a camera down the drain to locate the blockage and assess pipe condition.

2

Cleaning or Repair

We use hydro-jetting or augering to clear the line, or recommend trenchless repair if needed.

3

Final Inspection

We re-inspect with the camera to confirm the line is clear and functioning properly.

Common questions

How often should I have my drains cleaned?

We recommend a professional cleaning every 1-2 years to prevent major clogs. If you notice slow drains, call sooner.

What is trenchless sewer repair?

It's a method that repairs pipes without digging up your yard. We insert a liner or pipe bursting tool through existing access points.
