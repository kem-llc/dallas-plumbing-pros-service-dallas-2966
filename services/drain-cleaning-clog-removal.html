Drain Cleaning & Clog Removal

Clear drains and prevent future clogs with advanced techniques.

Schedule this service

Overview

Slow drains, gurgling sounds, and foul odors are signs of a clog. We use hydro-jetting and video inspection to thoroughly clean your drains and identify underlying issues. Our methods are safe for your pipes and effective against even tough blockages like grease and tree roots.

What's included

  • Hydro-jetting removes 100% of buildup
  • Camera inspection pinpoints problem areas
  • Prevents recurring clogs

How it works

1

Inspection

We insert a camera into the drain to locate the clog and assess pipe condition.

2

Clearing

Using a drain snake or hydro-jetter, we break up and flush away the blockage.

3

Final Check

We run water and re-inspect to confirm the drain is clear and flowing freely.

Common questions

Is hydro-jetting safe for old pipes?

Yes, hydro-jetting uses controlled pressure that is safe for most pipe materials, including PVC and cast iron.

How often should I have my drains cleaned?

We recommend professional drain cleaning every 12-18 months to prevent buildup and avoid emergencies.
