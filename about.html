Our story

Your Local Plumbing Experts Since 1995

To provide Dallas homeowners and businesses with reliable, honest, and affordable plumbing services – every time.

Dallas Plumbing Pros was founded in 1995 by master plumber John Smith with a simple mission: deliver quality plumbing service with integrity. Starting with a single truck, John built a reputation for showing up on time, fixing the problem right, and charging fair prices. Over the years, we've grown to a team of 20+ licensed plumbers, but our core values remain the same.

Today, we serve thousands of satisfied customers across Dallas and surrounding areas. We invest in ongoing training, modern equipment, and customer service excellence. Whether it's a minor faucet repair or a major sewer line replacement, we treat every job with the same dedication. We're proud to be a trusted part of the Dallas community.
