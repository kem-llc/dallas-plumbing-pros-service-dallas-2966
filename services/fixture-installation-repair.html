Fixture Installation & Repair

Install and repair faucets, toilets, sinks, and more.

Overview

Upgrade your kitchen or bathroom with new fixtures, or get your existing ones working like new. Our plumbers handle everything from leaky faucets to toilet replacements, ensuring a perfect fit and leak-free operation.

What's included

  • Wide selection of brands and styles
  • Precision installation for no drips or leaks
  • Toilet repair and replacement
  • Garbage disposal installation

How it works

1

Consultation

We discuss your needs, measure existing spaces, and recommend suitable fixtures.

2

Removal & Preparation

We carefully remove old fixtures and prepare the plumbing connections.

3

Installation & Testing

New fixtures are installed and tested for proper function and leaks.

Common questions

Can you install a faucet I bought online?

Yes, we can install any standard faucet. However, we recommend high-quality brands for longevity.

How much does it cost to replace a toilet?

Labor for a standard toilet replacement is typically $150-$250, plus the cost of the toilet. We'll provide a firm quote upfront.
